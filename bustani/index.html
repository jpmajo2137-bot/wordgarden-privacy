🌱 Bustani ya Maneno — Sera ya Faragha

Tarehe ya kuanza kutumika: 24 Oktoba 2026 · Programu: Bustani ya Maneno (com.wordgarden.sw) kwa Android na iOS · English version below

Kwa ufupi

  • Hakuna akaunti wala kuingia. Maendeleo yako yanahifadhiwa kwenye simu yako. Msanidi wa programu (developer) hakusanyi wala hahifadhi taarifa zako binafsi (kama jina au barua pepe), na hana seva inayopokea maendeleo yako.
  • Ili programu ibaki bure, inaonyesha matangazo ya Google AdMob. Ili kuonyesha na kupima matangazo, Google inaweza kukusanya na kuchakata kitambulisho cha matangazo (advertising ID), makadirio ya mahali ulipo (kutokana na anwani ya IP), taarifa za kifaa, na kama uliliona au kuligusa tangazo.
  • Hakuna matangazo wakati wa somo, kipimo cha msamiati, michezo wala unaposoma hadithi. Unaweza kukataa matangazo yaliyobinafsishwa (personalized ads), na vipengele vyote vya programu vitaendelea kufanya kazi.

1. Taarifa zinazohifadhiwa kwenye simu yako

Maendeleo yako ya kujifunza (hatua ya kila neno na wakati wa marudio yake yajayo), kumbukumbu za siku ulizojifunza, matokeo ya vipimo vya msamiati, maneno uliyoweka kwenye vipendwa, lugha unayojifunza, mipangilio yako, na idadi ya matangazo ya skrini nzima yaliyokwisha kuonyeshwa huhifadhiwa katika hifadhi ya programu ndani ya simu yako. Maendeleo ya kila lugha (Kiingereza na Kikorea) huhifadhiwa kando. Taarifa hizi hazitumwi kwa msanidi. Zinafutwa unapoondoa (uninstall) programu, au unapochagua «Futa maendeleo yote» katika Mipangilio ya programu (kwa lugha unayojifunza wakati huo). Ikiwa umewasha nakala rudufu ya mfumo wa simu yako (kwa mfano nakala rudufu ya Google au iCloud), huduma hiyo inaweza kujumuisha data ya programu hii; msanidi hana ufikiaji wa nakala hizo.

2. Nakala rudufu (backup)

Ukichagua «Hamisha nakala rudufu», programu hutengeneza faili ya JSON yenye maendeleo yako na kufungua menyu ya kushiriki ya simu yako. Wewe ndiye unayechagua mahali pa kuihifadhi au kuituma (kwa mfano Drive au Faili). Unaweza pia kunakili nakala rudufu kama msimbo na kuubandika mahali unapotaka. Programu yenyewe haitumi nakala rudufu popote.

3. Matamshi (sauti)

Programu inatumia injini ya kubadilisha maandishi kuwa sauti (text-to-speech) iliyo kwenye simu yako. Ni maandishi ya neno au sentensi tu yanayopelekwa kwenye injini hiyo. Maikrofoni haitumiki, na hakuna kinachorekodiwa. Injini hiyo inafuata sera ya faragha ya mtengenezaji wake (kwa mfano Google au Apple).

4. Matangazo (Google AdMob)

  • Yanapoonekana: tangazo dogo la bango (banner) sehemu ya chini ya skrini kuu, juu ya vichupo; na mara chache (si zaidi ya mara 2 kwa siku) tangazo la skrini nzima unaporudi bustanini baada ya kumaliza somo. Hakuna matangazo wakati wa somo, kipimo cha msamiati, michezo wala unaposoma hadithi.
  • Nani anayachakata: Matangazo hutolewa na Google AdMob (Google LLC). Ili kuchagua, kuonyesha na kupima matangazo, na kuzuia udanganyifu, Google inaweza kukusanya:
    • kitambulisho cha matangazo (advertising ID kwenye Android; kwenye iPhone, IDFA ikiwa tu umeruhusu ufuatiliaji). Hata kama hukuruhusu ufuatiliaji au umefuta kitambulisho cha matangazo, kitambulisho kinachotumika ndani ya programu za msanidi huyu pekee (app set ID kwenye Android) au ndani ya programu hii pekee (publisher first-party ID kwenye iPhone) kinaweza kutumika kudhibiti mara ngapi tangazo moja linaonyeshwa, kupima matangazo na kuzuia udanganyifu.
    • makadirio ya mahali ulipo, yanayotokana na anwani ya IP
    • taarifa za kifaa na programu (aina ya simu, mfumo wa uendeshaji, lugha, toleo), jinsi unavyoingiliana na matangazo, na taarifa za uchunguzi wa hitilafu (diagnostics)
  • Uchakataji nje ya nchi yako: Taarifa hizi zinaweza kuchakatwa kwenye seva za Google zilizo nje ya nchi yako (kwa mfano nchini Marekani). Soma Sera ya Faragha ya Google na jinsi Google inavyotumia taarifa kutoka kwa tovuti na programu zinazotumia huduma zake.
  • Daraja la matangazo: Matangazo yote yamewekewa kikomo cha daraja la General (G), yaani matangazo yanayofaa watu wa rika zote.

5. Chaguo lako kuhusu matangazo yaliyobinafsishwa

  • iPhone/iPad: Kabla matangazo hayajaanza, utaulizwa kama unairuhusu programu hii ikufuatilie («Allow Tracking»). Ukichagua «Ask App Not to Track», IDFA haitatumika. Unaweza kubadilisha chaguo hili wakati wowote katika Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Ikiwa tayari ulikataa kwenye skrini ya ridhaa iliyoelezwa hapa chini, hutaulizwa tena.
  • Android: Fungua Mipangilio (Settings) ya simu › Google › Matangazo (Ads); kwenye baadhi ya simu ni Faragha (Privacy) › Matangazo. Hapo unaweza kuweka upya au kufuta kitambulisho cha matangazo.
  • Maeneo yenye sheria za ridhaa: Ikiwa uko katika Eneo la Kiuchumi la Ulaya (EEA), Uingereza au Uswisi, skrini ya kuomba ridhaa yako itaonekana mwanzoni. Katika maeneo hayo na katika baadhi ya majimbo ya Marekani, unaweza kubadilisha chaguo lako wakati wowote katika Mipangilio ya programu › «Faragha ya matangazo». Kipengele hiki huonekana tu pale sheria inapokihitaji.
  • Hata ukikataa matangazo yaliyobinafsishwa, vipengele vyote vitaendelea kufanya kazi; matangazo yasiyobinafsishwa yanaweza kuonyeshwa.

6. Ruhusa (permissions)

Kwenye Android, programu inatumia ufikiaji wa intaneti na ruhusa ya advertising ID kwa ajili ya matangazo, pamoja na ruhusa ya mtetemo (vibration) kwa milio ya majibu. Kwenye iPhone, inaomba ruhusa ya ufuatiliaji tu kama ilivyoelezwa katika sehemu ya 5. Haiombi ruhusa nyeti kama mahali ulipo, anwani za watu (contacts), kamera, maikrofoni au hifadhi ya faili.

7. Wahusika wengine

Msanidi hauzi taarifa zozote binafsi. Taarifa zinazohusu matangazo hukusanywa na kuchakatwa moja kwa moja na Google AdMob kama ilivyoelezwa katika sehemu ya 4. Msanidi hupokea ripoti za jumla tu (kama idadi ya matangazo yaliyoonyeshwa), ambazo hazionyeshi wewe ni nani.

8. Watoto

Programu hii imekusudiwa watu wenye umri wa miaka 13 na zaidi, na hailengi watoto. Hatukusanyi kwa makusudi taarifa binafsi za watoto walio chini ya umri wa miaka 13. Matangazo yote yamewekewa kikomo cha daraja la General (G).

9. Mabadiliko

Sera hii ikibadilishwa, tutaweka toleo jipya kwenye ukurasa huu pamoja na tarehe mpya ya kuanza kutumika.

Bustani ya Maneno — Privacy Policy (English)

Effective date: October 24, 2026 · App: Bustani ya Maneno (com.wordgarden.sw) for Android and iOS

In short

  • There are no accounts or sign-in. Your progress is stored on your device. The developer does not collect or store your personal data (such as your name or email address), and has no server that receives your progress.
  • To keep the app free, it shows Google AdMob ads. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process your advertising ID, approximate location (derived from your IP address), device information, and whether you saw or tapped an ad.
  • There are no ads during lessons, the placement test, the games or while you read a story. You can refuse personalized ads, and every feature of the app keeps working.

1. Information stored on your device

Your learning progress (the stage of each word and when its next review is due), your study-day records, your placement test results, favourite words, the language you are learning, your settings and the number of full-screen ads already shown are stored in the app's storage on your device. Progress for each language (English and Korean) is kept separately. This information is not sent to the developer. It is deleted when you uninstall the app, or when you choose «Futa maendeleo yote» (Delete all progress) in the app's Settings (for the language you are learning at that time). If you have turned on your phone's system backup (for example Google backup or iCloud), that service may include this app's data; the developer has no access to those backups.

2. Backup

If you choose «Hamisha nakala rudufu» (Export backup), the app creates a JSON file with your progress and opens your phone's share menu. You choose where to save or send it (for example Drive or Files). You can also copy the backup as a code and paste it wherever you like. The app itself does not send the backup anywhere.

3. Pronunciation (audio)

The app uses the text-to-speech engine on your device. Only the text of a word or sentence is passed to that engine. The microphone is never used, and nothing is recorded. The engine follows its maker's privacy policy (for example Google or Apple).

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

  • Where ads appear: a small banner at the bottom of the main screens, just above the tabs; and occasionally (at most twice a day) a full-screen ad when you return to the garden after finishing a lesson. There are no ads during lessons, the placement test, the games or while you read a story.
  • Who processes the data: Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To select, serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:
    • your advertising identifier (the advertising ID on Android; on iPhone, the IDFA only if you allow tracking). Even if you do not allow tracking or have deleted your advertising ID, an identifier limited to this developer's apps (app set ID on Android) or to this app (publisher first-party ID on iPhone) may be used for frequency capping, ad measurement and fraud prevention.
    • approximate location, derived from your IP address
    • device and app information (model, operating system, language, version), ad interactions, and diagnostic information
  • Processing outside your country: This information may be processed on Google's servers outside your country (for example in the United States). See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Ad rating: All ads are limited to the General (G) rating, meaning ads suitable for all ages.

5. Your choices about personalized ads

  • iPhone/iPad: Before ads start, you are asked whether to allow this app to track you ("Allow Tracking"). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not used. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you already refused on the consent screen described below, you are not asked again.
  • Android: Open your phone's Settings › Google › Ads; on some phones it is Settings › Privacy › Ads. There you can reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Regions with consent laws: If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK or Switzerland, a consent screen appears at the start. In those regions and in some US states, you can change your choice at any time in the app's Settings › «Faragha ya matangazo» (Ad privacy). This item appears only where the law requires it.
  • Even if you refuse personalized ads, every feature keeps working; non-personalized ads may be shown.

6. Permissions

On Android, the app uses internet access and the advertising ID permission for ads, plus the vibration permission for answer feedback. On iPhone, it asks only for tracking permission, as described in section 5. It does not request sensitive permissions such as location, contacts, camera, microphone or file storage.

7. Third parties

The developer does not sell any personal data. Ad-related information is collected and processed directly by Google AdMob as described in section 4. The developer only receives aggregate reports (such as how many ads were shown) that do not identify you.

8. Children

This app is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. All ads are limited to the General (G) rating.

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
