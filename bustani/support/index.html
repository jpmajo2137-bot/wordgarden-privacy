Maswali ya mara kwa mara · FAQ

Je, ninahitaji akaunti? · Do I need an account?
Hapana. Hakuna akaunti wala kuingia. · No. No account or login.

Nitabadilishaje lugha ninayojifunza? · How do I switch between English and Korean?
Gusa jina la lugha chini ya kichwa kwenye skrini kuu, au nenda Mipangilio → Lugha unayojifunza. Maendeleo ya kila lugha huhifadhiwa kando. · Tap the language under the title on the home screen, or go to Settings → Lugha unayojifunza. Each language keeps its own progress.

Je, kuna matangazo? · Are there ads?
Ili programu ibaki bure, kuna bango dogo chini ya vichupo vikuu na, mara 2 kwa siku zaidi, tangazo la skrini nzima baada ya kumaliza somo. Hakuna matangazo wakati unajifunza. · To keep the app free there is a small banner at the bottom of the main tabs and, at most twice a day, a full-screen ad after you finish a lesson. Never while you study.

Maendeleo yangu yanahifadhiwa wapi? · Where is my progress saved?
Kwenye simu yako tu (programu huweka pia nakala ya kiotomatiki kwenye simu hiyo hiyo). Kabla ya kubadilisha simu, tumia Mipangilio → Hifadhi nakala rudufu. Kwenye simu mpya, fungua programu na uguse «Nina nakala rudufu» kwenye skrini ya kwanza. · Only on your phone (the app also keeps an automatic copy on the same phone). Before changing phones, use Settings → Hifadhi nakala rudufu; on the new phone, open the app and tap «Nina nakala rudufu» on the very first screen.

Naweza kupata kikumbusho cha kila siku? · Can I get a daily reminder?
Ndiyo. Washa Mipangilio → Kikumbusho cha kujifunza na uchague saa. Kikumbusho hakiji siku ambayo umeshamaliza lengo lako. · Yes. Turn on Settings → Kikumbusho cha kujifunza and pick a time. No reminder comes on days you have already finished your goal.

Sisikii sauti. · I can't hear the audio.
Programu inatumia sauti za simu yako (text-to-speech). Hakikisha sauti ya Kiingereza au Kikorea imesakinishwa na simu haiko kimya. · The app uses your phone's text-to-speech voices; make sure an English/Korean voice is installed.

Inafanya kazi bila intaneti? · Does it work offline?
Ndiyo — maneno na hadithi zote ziko ndani ya programu. · Yes — all words and stories are inside the app.
