Maswali ya mara kwa mara · FAQ

Je, ninahitaji akaunti? · Do I need an account?
Hapana. Hakuna akaunti wala kuingia. · No. No account or login.

Nitabadilishaje lugha ninayojifunza? · How do I switch between English and Korean?
Gusa jina la lugha chini ya kichwa kwenye skrini kuu, au fungua Mipangilio (ikoni ya gia): mstari wa kwanza ni Lugha unayojifunza, na ukigusa Badilisha inafunguka Chagua lugha ya kujifunza. Maendeleo ya kila lugha huhifadhiwa kando. · Tap the language under the title on the home screen, or open Settings (the gear icon): the first row is Lugha unayojifunza, and Badilisha opens Chagua lugha ya kujifunza. Each language keeps its own progress.

Je, kuna matangazo? · Are there ads?
Ili programu ibaki bure, kuna bango dogo chini ya vichupo vikuu, na tangazo la skrini nzima baada ya kumaliza somo, lisilozidi mara 2 kwa siku. Hakuna matangazo wakati unajifunza. · To keep the app free there is a small banner at the bottom of the main tabs and, at most twice a day, a full-screen ad after you finish a lesson. Never while you study.

Maendeleo yangu yanahifadhiwa wapi? · Where is my progress saved?
Kwenye simu yako tu (programu huweka pia nakala ya kiotomatiki kwenye simu hiyo hiyo). Kabla ya kubadilisha simu, tumia Mipangilio → Hifadhi nakala rudufu. Kwenye simu mpya, fungua programu na uguse «Nina nakala rudufu — rejesha maendeleo yangu» kwenye skrini ya kwanza. · Only on your phone (the app also keeps an automatic copy on the same phone). Before changing phones, use Settings → Hifadhi nakala rudufu; on the new phone, open the app and tap «Nina nakala rudufu — rejesha maendeleo yangu» on the very first screen.

Ni nakala rudufu zipi ninazoweza kuingiza? · Which backups can I load?

  • Nakala rudufu ya kozi ileile hurejesha kozi hiyo. · A backup of the same course restores that course.
  • Nakala rudufu ya kozi nyingine inayofundisha lugha ileile (kwa mfano English → Korean na Vietnamese → Korean) inaweza kuingizwa kwenye kozi unayosoma. Maneno na maendeleo yako yanafuata; sura za hadithi ulizokwisha kusoma hazifuati. · A backup of another course that teaches the same language (for example English → Korean and Vietnamese → Korean) can be loaded into the course you are studying. Your words and progress come along; chapters already read in the story do not.
  • Nakala rudufu ya kozi inayofundisha lugha tofauti hurejeshwa kwenye kozi yake yenyewe. Programu huuliza kwanza, kisha hubadilisha hadi kozi hiyo. · A backup of a course that teaches a different language is restored into its own course. The app asks first, then switches to that course.
  • Kwenye skrini ya kwanza kabisa, unapochagua kozi, pia kuna kiungo «Nina nakala rudufu — rejesha maendeleo yangu». · On the very first screen, where you choose your course, there is also a link, «Nina nakala rudufu — rejesha maendeleo yangu».

Naweza kupata kikumbusho cha kila siku? · Can I get a daily reminder?
Ndiyo. Washa Mipangilio → Kikumbusho cha kujifunza na uchague saa. Kikumbusho hakiji siku ambayo umeshamaliza lengo lako. · Yes. Turn on Settings → Kikumbusho cha kujifunza and pick a time. No reminder comes on days you have already finished your goal.

Sisikii sauti. · I can't hear the audio.
Programu inatumia sauti za simu yako (text-to-speech). Hakikisha sauti ya Kiingereza au Kikorea imesakinishwa na simu haiko kimya. · The app uses your phone's text-to-speech voices; make sure an English/Korean voice is installed.

Inafanya kazi bila intaneti? · Does it work offline?
Ndiyo — maneno na hadithi zote ziko ndani ya programu. · Yes — all words and stories are inside the app.
