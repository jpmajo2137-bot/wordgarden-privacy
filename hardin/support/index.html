FAQ

Do I need an account? · Kailangan ba ng account?
No. No account or login. · Hindi. Walang account o login.

Are there ads? · May ads ba?
To keep the app free, there is a small banner at the bottom of the main tabs and, at most twice a day, a full-screen ad after you finish a study session. There are never ads while you study. In the EEA/UK you can change your ad consent in Settings → Privacy ng ads. · Para manatiling libre, may maliit na banner sa ibaba ng mga tab at, hanggang 2 beses bawat araw, isang full-screen ad pagkatapos ng aralin. Walang ads habang nag-aaral.

Where is my progress saved? · Saan naka-save ang progreso ko?
Only on your phone. Before changing phones or uninstalling, use Settings → Export backup to save a backup file or code, then Import backup on the new phone. · Sa phone mo lang. Bago magpalit ng phone, gamitin ang Settings → I-export ang backup, at I-import sa bagong phone.

How do I change my starting level? · Paano palitan ang antas?
Go to Settings to re-take the 2-minute level test or pick a level yourself. Your progress stays. · Sa Settings, puwedeng sukatin ulit ang antas o pumili ng antas. Hindi mawawala ang progreso.

I can't hear the audio. · Walang tunog.
The app uses your phone's text-to-speech voices. Check that an English (or Korean) voice is installed and the phone isn't on silent. · Gumagamit ang app ng text-to-speech ng phone mo; tiyaking may English/Korean na boses at hindi naka-silent.

Does it work offline? · Gumagana ba offline?
Yes — all words and stories are inside the app. · Oo — nasa loob ng app ang lahat ng salita at kuwento.
