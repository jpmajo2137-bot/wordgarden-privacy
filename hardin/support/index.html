FAQ

Do I need an account? · Kailangan ba ng account?
No. No account or login. · Hindi. Walang account o login.

Are there ads? · May ads ba?
To keep the app free, there is a small banner at the bottom of the main tabs and, at most twice a day, a full-screen ad after you finish a study session. There are never ads while you study. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland a consent screen appears first, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy ng ads. In some US states, where that row appears, you can use it to opt out of personalized ads. The row appears only where it is needed. · Para manatiling libre, may maliit na banner sa ibaba ng mga tab at, hanggang 2 beses bawat araw, isang full-screen ad pagkatapos ng aralin. Walang ads habang nag-aaral. Sa EEA, UK at Switzerland, may consent screen sa simula, at mababago mo ito sa Settings → Privacy ng ads. Sa ilang estado sa US, kung makikita mo ang row na ito, doon ka puwedeng tumanggi sa personalized ads.

Where is my progress saved? · Saan naka-save ang progreso ko?
Only on your phone. Before changing phones or uninstalling, use Settings → I-export ang backup to save a backup file or code, then Mag-import ng backup on the new phone. · Sa phone mo lang. Bago magpalit ng phone, gamitin ang Settings → I-export ang backup, at Mag-import ng backup sa bagong phone.

What are “Awtomatikong backup” and “Ibalik sa dati”? · Ano ang awtomatikong backup?
The Android and iPhone apps automatically keep one more copy of your progress on your phone and use it to restore your progress if the phone runs low on space or the app's saved data gets damaged. You can see when it was last saved in Settings → Data. When an earlier copy exists, Ibalik sa dati takes you back to it (usually the end of your previous study day); progress made after that is removed. The copy stays on your phone and the app never sends it anywhere, so it is deleted when you uninstall the app or use “Burahin ang lahat ng progreso”. Before changing phones, use I-export ang backup or Kopyahin ang backup code. · Kusang nagse-save ang app ng isa pang kopya ng progreso mo sa phone, para maibalik ito kapag naubos ang storage o nasira ang data ng app. Ibinabalik ng Ibalik sa dati ang naunang kopya; mawawala ang progreso mula noon. Nasa phone mo lang ang kopyang ito at nabubura kapag in-uninstall ang app, kaya bago magpalit ng phone, gamitin ang I-export ang backup o Kopyahin ang backup code.

How do I change my starting level? · Paano palitan ang antas?
Go to Settings → Sukat ng bokabularyo › Sukatin to re-take the 2-minute level test, or pick a level yourself in Settings → Antas. Your progress stays. · Sa Settings → Sukat ng bokabularyo › Sukatin, puwedeng sukatin ulit ang bokabularyo mo, o pumili ng antas sa Settings → Antas. Hindi mawawala ang progreso.

Can the app remind me to study? · Puwede ba akong paalalahanan ng app?
Yes. Turn on Settings → Paalala sa pag-aaral and pick a time. On days when there is something to study, you get one reminder with how many words to water and new seeds to plant. You can also turn it on with “Oo, paalalahanan ako” when the reminder card appears in the Hardin tab. The app asks for notification permission only when you turn it on; to stop, switch the same row off. · Oo. I-on ang Settings → Paalala sa pag-aaral at pumili ng oras. Isang paalala bawat araw kapag may dapat pag-aralan. Hihingi lang ng pahintulot para sa notifications kapag binuksan mo ito; i-off lang ulit para itigil.

My reminder didn't come. · Hindi dumating ang paalala.
There is no reminder on a day when you have already finished your goal or there is nothing to study. Otherwise, check that notifications for the app are allowed in your phone's Settings (if they are off, the Paalala sa pag-aaral row says so), and that battery saver or battery optimization isn't blocking the app (on Android, set its battery use to “Unrestricted”). Reminders may arrive a few minutes late, and they pause if you don't open the app for a week. · Walang paalala kapag tapos na ang layunin mo sa araw na iyon o walang dapat pag-aralan. Tiyaking pinapayagan ang notifications ng app sa Settings ng phone, at hindi ito hinaharangan ng battery saver o battery optimization. Maaaring mahuli nang ilang minuto ang paalala, at titigil ito kapag hindi mo binuksan ang app nang isang linggo.

I can't hear the audio. · Walang tunog.
The app uses your phone's text-to-speech voices. Check that an English (or Korean) voice is installed and the phone isn't on silent. · Gumagamit ang app ng text-to-speech ng phone mo; tiyaking may English/Korean na boses at hindi naka-silent.

A card says there is no English (or Korean) voice. · May card na nagsasabing walang boses.
It appears on the Hardin tab and in Settings when your phone has no text-to-speech voice for the language you are learning; until a voice is installed, pronunciation is off and listening questions are skipped. On Android, tap I-install ang boses, download the English (or Korean) voice and come back to the app; it checks again by itself (or tap Suriin ulit in Settings). On iPhone/iPad, go to Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices and download the voice. · Lumalabas ito kapag walang English/Korean na boses ang phone mo, kaya nilalaktawan ang pakikinig. Sa Android, pindutin ang I-install ang boses, i-download ang boses at bumalik sa app. Sa iPhone/iPad, pumunta sa Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices at i-download ang boses.

Does it work offline? · Gumagana ba offline?
Yes — all words and stories are inside the app. · Oo — nasa loob ng app ang lahat ng salita at kuwento.

How do I send feedback? · Paano magpadala ng puna?
In Settings → Magpadala ng puna, tap “Mag-email”. Your mail app opens with our address and a subject (app name, version and edition) already filled in; just write your message and send it. If no mail app opens, email the address above. · Sa Settings → Magpadala ng puna, pindutin ang “Mag-email”. Bubukas ang email app mo na may address at subject na; isulat lang ang mensahe at ipadala.

How do I rate the app? · Paano i-rate ang app?
Go to Settings → I-rate ang app › Mag-review. On iPhone and iPad it opens the App Store review page; on Android it opens the app's Google Play page. Your rating and review help us a lot. · Sa Settings → I-rate ang app › Mag-review. Sa iPhone/iPad, bubukas ang review page sa App Store; sa Android, ang page ng app sa Google Play. Malaking tulong sa amin ang rating at review mo.

Hardin ng Salita: Korean

Where is the Korean alphabet? · Nasaan ang alpabeto ng Korean?
Open the Salita tab and tap Alpabeto ng Korean (Hangul 101) at the top of the list, or go to the Laro tab › Hangul 101. The Alpabeto tab shows all 24 letters in dictionary order; tap a letter to hear its name. The other tabs teach consonants, vowels, batchim and building syllables, with a quiz. · Sa tab na Salita, pindutin ang Alpabeto ng Korean (Hangul 101) sa itaas ng listahan, o pumunta sa tab na Laro › Hangul 101. Nasa Alpabeto ang 24 na titik; pindutin ang titik para marinig ang pangalan nito.

How do I hide the romanization? · Paano itago ang romanisasyon?
Turn off Settings → Tunog at Pagbasa › Romanisasyon. Turn it on again to show the romanization next to Korean words. · I-off ang Settings → Tunog at Pagbasa › Romanisasyon; i-on ulit para makita ang romanisasyon.

Is this an official EPS-TOPIK or TOPIK reviewer? · Opisyal ba ito?
No. Hardin ng Salita: Korean is an independent app and is not affiliated with HRD Korea, the Department of Migrant Workers (DMW) or NIIED (TOPIK). It is a vocabulary app, not a mock test. · Hindi. Independent na app ito at walang kaugnayan sa HRD Korea, DMW o NIIED (TOPIK). Vocabulary app ito, hindi mock test.
