WordGarden: Korean

Learn Korean words, starting from Hangul

  • 15,000+ Korean words in 7 levels, each with an English meaning, an example sentence with its translation, and audio
  • Hangul 101: the 24 letters with their names and sounds, batchim, a syllable builder, a quiz and reading practice
  • Romanization you can switch off, and memory tips from Hanja and word parts: 학생 = 학 (學, learn) + 생 (生, person)
  • A 2-minute level test, then reviews spaced 1 → 3 → 7 → 15 → 35 → 90 days apart
  • A Year in Seoul: a 15-chapter story about Sam, plus 3 word games
  • Meanings in US or British spelling, to match your phone
  • No account · Works offline · Your progress stays on your phone

Using an Android phone?

WordGarden: Korean is in a closed test on Google Play. Before it can be released to everyone, at least 12 testers need to stay in the test for 14 days in a row. Joining takes about 5 minutes.

  1. Join the tester group. Sign in with the Google account you use on the Play Store, then tap “Join group”.
  2. Open the testing page and tap “Become a tester”.
  3. Install the app from Google Play.
  4. Keep it installed for 14 days and open it now and then. If you leave the group or the test during that time, your 14 days start again.

If you see “You don't have permission”, you are not signed in to Google: tap Sign in at the top of the page, then open the group link again.

If the Play Store says “Item not found”, wait 10–30 minutes and open step 3 again: Google needs a little time to add you as a tester.
