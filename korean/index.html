🌱 WordGarden: Korean — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026 · App: WordGarden: Korean (com.wordgarden.en.korean; home-screen name “WordGarden”) for Android and iPhone/iPad, whatever name it is listed under on Google Play or the App Store · Developer: Joung Hun Park (박종훈)

In short

  • No account, no sign-in. Your progress is stored on your device. The app does not send personal data (such as your name or email address) to the developer, and the developer has no server that stores your data.
  • The app shows Google AdMob ads. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process your device's advertising identifier, approximate location (derived from your IP address), device information, and whether you saw or tapped an ad.
  • There are no ads while you study, take the vocabulary test, play a game or read a story. You can refuse personalized ads, and every feature keeps working.
  • Study reminders are optional and scheduled on your device only. If you email us (including with “Send feedback” in Settings), we use your email only to reply.

1. Information stored on your device

The app keeps, in its private storage on your device: your learning progress (the stage of each word and when its next review is due), the days you studied, your streak and watering cans, vocabulary test results, starred words, chapters read, game records, your settings (including the reminder time and whether romanization is shown), how many full-screen ads were shown that day, and a few small app notes, such as when the app last showed the rating window, or the app's most recent error so you can choose to include it in a feedback email (section 9). Google's consent form (section 5) stores your ad consent choices on the device.

Auto-backup: The app also keeps a spare copy of your progress in its private folder on the device (plus one older copy used by “Restore an earlier copy” in Settings), so your progress can be recovered if the phone runs out of space or the app's data gets damaged. You don't see this file, and the app never sends it off the device.

Your phone's own backup: If you have turned on iCloud Backup or your phone's Google backup, that backup may include the app's data and the auto-backup. It is managed by your Apple or Google account; the developer has no access to it.

None of this is sent to a server of the developer. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. “Reset all progress” in Settings deletes your progress, settings and the auto-backup and cannot be undone (backup files you exported yourself, and small notes such as the ad count, may remain).

2. Backup files, backup codes and sharing

“Export backup” creates a JSON file with your learning data and settings and opens your phone's share sheet; you choose where to save or send it (for example Drive or the Files app). “Copy backup code” copies the same data to the clipboard. Neither contains your name, email address or advertising identifier. The app never sends a backup anywhere by itself. “Import backup” and “Load backup code” read only the file or code you choose.

“Share result” on your vocabulary test result, “Tell a friend” in Settings and “Tell a friend” when you reach a streak milestone open your phone's share sheet; you choose what to send and to whom. The message is a short text written by the app (when you share a test result or a streak, it includes your estimated vocabulary size and level, or your number of days in a row), with a link to the app's download page on this website and a short code saying where it was shared from (for example ?c=invite). It contains no name, email address or identifier of yours, and the app does not send what you share to the developer.

3. Pronunciation (audio)

The app uses the text-to-speech engine on your device. Only the text of the word or sentence to be read is passed to that engine. The app never uses the microphone and records nothing. Voice data (when you tap “Install voice” or download a voice in your phone's settings) is downloaded by your device's engine, which follows its maker's privacy policy (for example Google, Samsung or Apple).

Whether meanings are shown in US or British spelling is decided on the device from your phone's language and region settings; nothing is sent anywhere for this.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

  • Where ads appear: a small banner at the bottom of the main screens, just above the tabs; and occasionally a full-screen ad when you finish a study session of at least 8 answers and tap to return to the garden from its summary (at most twice a day, at least 10 minutes apart, and never on your first day of study). There are no ads while you study, take the vocabulary test, play a game or read a story.
  • Who processes the data: Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To select, serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:
    • your advertising identifier (the advertising ID on Android; on iPhone, the IDFA only if you allow tracking). Even if you do not allow tracking or have deleted your advertising ID, an identifier limited to this developer's apps (app set ID on Android) or to this app (publisher first-party ID on iPhone) may be used for frequency capping, ad measurement and fraud prevention
    • approximate location, derived from your IP address
    • device and app information (model, operating system, language, version), ad interactions and diagnostic information
  • How it is collected: The Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app collects this information and sends it to Google over an encrypted connection when the ad service starts and each time the app requests or shows an ad.
  • How long it is kept: As set by Google. See how Google retains data.
  • Ad rating: All ads are limited to the General (G) rating, meaning ads suitable for all ages.

5. Your choices about personalized ads

  • iPhone/iPad: Before ads start, you are asked whether to allow this app to track you. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the IDFA is not used. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you already refused on the consent form described below, the app does not ask.
  • Android: In your phone's Settings › Google › Ads (on some phones Settings › Privacy › Ads), you can reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Before ads are first loaded, Google's consent form appears. You can change your choice at any time in the app's Settings › “Ad privacy”.
  • US states with privacy laws: No form appears at launch. If you see “Ad privacy” in the app's Settings, you can use it to opt out of the sale or sharing of your data for personalized ads. The row appears only where the law requires it.
  • Everywhere else: Google's consent form does not appear. You can limit ads at any time with your phone's settings above (the tracking choice on iPhone/iPad, the advertising ID on Android).
  • Personalized ads may be chosen using interests that Google infers about you. Refusing personalized ads, or limiting ads in your phone's settings, does not change any feature of the app; non-personalized ads may still be shown.
  • If you don't want any data sent for ads, you can use the app offline: every word, story and game is inside the app.

6. Permissions

Android: The app itself uses internet access (INTERNET), for ads (learning works offline; links you tap, such as this policy, email or the app store, open in a browser or another app), and the vibration permission (VIBRATE), for a short buzz when you answer wrong or tap a button. The reminder (notification) library adds the notification permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, on Android 13 and later, asked only when you turn on reminders), and RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED and WAKE_LOCK so reminders still arrive while the phone is idle or after it restarts. Google's ads library (Google Mobile Ads SDK) adds the advertising ID permission (AD_ID); ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS (Android's ad services); the network state permission (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE); and FOREGROUND_SERVICE, through the background-task library it uses (androidx.work). The app does not use the exact-alarm permission.

iPhone/iPad: The app may ask two things: whether to allow tracking for ads (section 5), and “Allow Notifications” when you turn on reminders.

On both, the app does not request sensitive permissions such as location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage.

7. Daily study reminders (optional)

Turn reminders on in Settings › Study reminder, or with the reminder card on the Garden tab, and pick a time. The app schedules up to 7 days of reminders ahead as local notifications on your device. The text is created on the device (words to water, new seeds, your streak and watering cans, sometimes one word to recall) and contains no ads. Nothing is sent to the developer or any server for this. You can turn reminders off at any time in the app's or the phone's settings.

8. Third parties and international transfers

The developer does not sell personal data. Ad-related information is collected and processed directly by Google AdMob as described in section 4; the developer receives only aggregate reports (such as how many ads were shown) that do not identify you.

  • Recipient: Google LLC (United States). For contact details, see the Google Privacy Policy.
  • What and why: the information in section 4, to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud
  • Where: on Google's servers in the United States and other countries
  • When and how: when the app uses the ad service (section 4), over an encrypted connection
  • How long: as set by Google (section 4)
  • How to refuse: see section 5

See also how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Your device's text-to-speech engine, your phone's own backup, the share sheet, your mail app and the rating window are part of your phone and follow their makers' privacy policies.

9. Email, feedback and word reports

“Send feedback” › “Write email” in Settings opens your own mail app with our address and a subject (app name, version and edition) already filled in. Below the space for your message, the draft includes the app name and version, your device's browser information (the user agent, which shows the device type and OS version) and the app's most recent error from the last 14 days, if there was one. The link “Something wrong with this word? Tell us” on a word card fills in what that card shows: the word, its number and level, meaning, example, translation and tip. A draft never contains your learning progress, settings or advertising identifier. You can read, edit and delete every line before sending, and you decide whether to send it.

When you send it, we receive your email address, your display name (if your mail app includes it) and your message. We use them only to reply and fix problems, never for anything else, and we don't give them to anyone. If you send us a Google account (Gmail) address to join the Android test, it is used only to add you to the tester list.

10. Ratings

Now and then, at a good moment (for example after a study session with many right answers, when a retaken vocabulary test shows your vocabulary has grown, when you complete a story chapter or set a new game record), the app may open the operating system's rating window, at most 3 times a year; no full-screen ad is shown that time. Whether it actually appears is decided by Apple or Google. A rating or review you send goes to Apple or Google under their terms; the developer sees it only as it appears on the store, under the name you use there.

11. Children

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (under 14 in Korea). All ads are limited to the General (G) rating. If a parent learns that their child has emailed us, please contact us and we will delete that email.

12. How long data is kept, and deletion

  • Data on your device (sections 1 and 2): stays on your device until you uninstall the app or use “Reset all progress”. You manage the backup files you export and your phone's own backup.
  • Emails (feedback, questions, test sign-ups): kept only as long as needed to reply or handle your request, and deleted as soon as you ask. Test sign-up addresses are removed from the tester list when the test ends or when you ask. They are deleted permanently from the mailbox and the list.
  • Ad data: kept by Google as set by Google (section 4).

13. Security

  • The developer has no server that stores personal data.
  • Your progress is kept in the app's private storage, which the operating system protects from other apps.
  • The Google ads SDK sends data over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Emails we receive are kept in a mailbox that only the developer manages.

14. Your rights

  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and the UK, US state privacy laws, or other national privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, to opt out of its sale or sharing, and to withdraw consent. The only personal data the developer holds is email you sent us; write to the address in section 17 and we will handle your request promptly.
  • Your progress on the device is in your hands: see it in the Progress tab, export it with “Export backup”, and delete it with “Reset all progress” or by uninstalling the app.
  • For ads: use the choices in section 5 to withdraw consent or to reset or delete your advertising ID. For data Google has already processed: if you have a Google Account, use My Ad Center and your Google Account's data controls; otherwise, follow the Google Privacy Policy to ask Google directly.

15. Complaints

You can contact us first (section 17). You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live (for example the ICO in the UK, your national authority in the EU, or the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner). The developer is based in Korea; there, complaints go to the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr, phone 118) and the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (www.kopico.go.kr).
