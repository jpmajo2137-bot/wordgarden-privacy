FAQ

Settings is the gear icon at the top right of the screen.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no sign-in.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your phone. The app has no server that stores your progress. It also keeps a spare copy on the same phone (see “Auto-backup” below). So before you change phones or delete the app, make a backup yourself.

How do I back up my progress?

Go to Settings › Data and choose one of these:

  • Export backup: the app creates a .json file and opens the share sheet. Save the file to Drive or the Files app, or send it to yourself by email or chat, then check that it was really saved.
  • Copy backup code: the app copies your progress as a code. Paste it into a note or a chat with yourself to keep it safe.

“Last backup” in Settings shows when you last backed up. Backup files and codes contain only your learning data and settings, not your name or email address.

How do I restore a backup?

Go to Settings › Data, choose Import backup and pick the .json file, or choose Load backup code, long-press the box to paste your code and tap Load. Before anything changes, the app shows how many words and days of study the backup has. The backup replaces all the progress on this phone.

Backups only work in the same app: a WordGarden: Korean backup can be loaded only in WordGarden: Korean.

I'm getting a new phone. How do I keep my garden?

  1. On the old phone: Settings › Data › Export backup (or Copy backup code), and send the file or code to the new phone, for example through Drive, email or a chat with yourself.
  2. On the new phone: install WordGarden, open it and go through the first steps (any level is fine; it will be replaced).
  3. Go to Settings › Data › Import backup (or Load backup code › Load).

This works between Android and iPhone too. Don't delete the app from the old phone until the new one has your progress.

What are “Auto-backup” and “Restore an earlier copy”?

The app automatically keeps a spare copy of your progress on the same phone and uses it to recover your progress if the phone runs out of space or the app's data gets damaged. Settings › Data shows when it was last saved. When an older copy exists, Restore an earlier copy takes you back to it (usually the end of your previous study day); anything after that is lost. The spare copy stays on that phone and is deleted with the app, so you still need Export backup when you change phones.

I deleted the app or reset my progress by mistake. Can I get it back?

If you exported a backup file or code, restore it as above. If not, we can't recover it for you, because your progress is never sent to a server. On some phones, if your phone's Google or iCloud backup is on, the app's data may come back when you reinstall it, but this isn't guaranteed. “Reset all progress” also deletes the auto-backup and can't be undone.

Can the app remind me to study?

Yes. Turn on Settings › Study reminder and pick a Reminder time, or tap Remind me when the reminder card appears on the Garden tab. You get one reminder a day, only when there's something to study (words to water, new seeds to plant), and none once you've reached that day's goal. The app asks for notification permission only when you turn it on; to stop, switch the same row off.

My reminder didn't come.

  • There's no reminder on a day when you've already reached your goal or there's nothing to study.
  • Check that notifications for the app are allowed in your phone's settings. If they are off, the Study reminder row in the app says so.
  • On Android, battery saving can block reminders: in your phone's Settings › Apps › WordGarden › Battery, choose “Unrestricted” (names differ by phone).
  • Reminders may arrive a few minutes late. The app schedules 7 days ahead; if you don't open it for a week, reminders pause until you open it again.

I can't hear the audio.

WordGarden uses your phone's own text-to-speech voices. Check the volume and silent mode, and make sure a Korean voice is installed (next question).

The app says there is no Korean voice. How do I install one?

This card appears on the Garden tab and in Settings when your phone has no Korean text-to-speech voice. Until one is installed, the app can't read Korean aloud and skips listening questions.

  • Android: tap Install voice. Your phone's text-to-speech engine (usually Google's or Samsung's) opens its voice data screen; download Korean (Wi‑Fi is best) and come back to the app. The app checks again by itself, or tap Check again in Settings. If the button doesn't open anything: open your phone's Settings, search for “Text-to-speech”, and in the engine's settings tap Install voice data and download Korean.
  • iPhone/iPad: go to Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices, choose Korean and download a voice.

Voice data comes from your phone's text-to-speech engine, not from WordGarden. The app only passes the text to be read to that engine on your phone; it never uses the microphone and records nothing.

How do I hide the romanization?

Turn off Settings › Sound & reading › Romanization. Turn it on again to show romanization next to Korean words.

Where is Hangul 101?

Open the Words tab and tap The Korean alphabet at the top of the list, or go to the Play tab › Hangul 101. It has Alphabet (all 24 letters in dictionary order; tap a letter to hear its name), Consonants, Vowels, Batchim, Build syllables, Quiz and Read.

Why do the meanings use US (or British) spelling?

Meanings follow your phone's language and region settings: US spelling (color, center) on phones set to the US or the Philippines, British spelling (colour, centre) on most other English-language phones. Change your phone's region to switch.

How do I change my level or retake the vocabulary test?

Go to Settings › Vocabulary test › Take the test to retake the 2-minute test, or pick a level yourself in Settings › Level. Your progress stays.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every word, story and game is inside the app.

Are there ads?

Yes, Google AdMob ads keep the app free. A small banner appears at the bottom of the main screens, just above the tabs. Sometimes a full-screen ad appears when you finish a study session of at least 8 answers and tap to return to the garden: at most twice a day, at least 10 minutes apart, and never on your first day of study. There are no ads while you study, take the vocabulary test, play a game or read a story, and all ads are rated for general audiences (G).

To choose about personalized ads: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears before ads are first loaded, and you can change your choice any time in Settings › Ad privacy. In some US states, Settings › Ad privacy lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your data for personalized ads (the row appears only where it is needed). Everywhere, you can limit ads with your phone's settings: on iPhone/iPad, Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking; on Android, Settings › Google › Ads (on some phones Settings › Privacy › Ads), where you can reset or delete your advertising ID. Refusing personalized ads or limiting ads does not change any feature of the app.

Does the app collect my data?

The app itself doesn't: your progress stays on your phone, and the developer has no server and no analytics. To show and measure ads, Google (AdMob) may collect your advertising identifier (on iPhone only if you allow tracking), approximate location from your IP address, device information and how you interact with ads. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I tell a friend about the app?

Go to Settings › Tell a friend › Share. Your friend gets a link to the download page, which shows the right steps for their phone.

How do I send feedback or report a wrong word?

Go to Settings › Send feedback › Write email. Your mail app opens with our address and a subject (app name and version) filled in, plus a little technical information to help us fix problems (version, device type, recent errors). You can delete it before sending. To report a word, open its card, tap Something wrong with this word? Tell us and tick what is wrong. If no mail app opens, email the address at the top of this page.
